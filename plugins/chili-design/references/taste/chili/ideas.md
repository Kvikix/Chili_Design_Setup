# Idea bank — interesting solutions from Chili projects

Fuel for `creative.md`, not a style to copy. **Opt-in**: read when the designer picks «Chili projects» as a reference, asks for bolder/more creative options, or a variant needs a «signature moment».
How to use: take the **principle**, not the look. Remix it into the current product's DS and domain; never transfer one client's brand, assets or copy to another. Cite as «(Chili: <project>)».

## Metaphor in components
- **Bag-handle cart sheet** — the cart bottom sheet has a shopping-bag handle shape on top (Chili: Ganbei). Principle: one domain object shapes one component. Remix: ticket-stub edge for bookings, envelope flap for messages, folder tab for documents.
- **Stories-style progress** in a questionnaire header (Chili: Balcia). Principle: borrow a familiar pattern from another app category. Remix: progress as route dots for travel, as stamps for loyalty.

## Making abstract values tangible
- **«≈ for 10 people»** next to cake weight; presets «3 kg — for 15 people» (Chili: Ganbei). Remix: «≈ 2 coffees a month» for a subscription, «≈ 40 min walk» for distance.
- **«12€ away from free delivery»** with the CTA itself acting as a progress bar to the minimum order (Chili: Ganbei). Remix: progress to next tier, to goal savings.
- **«You will receive N securities…»** live under the amount input (Chili: Twino). Remix: show the outcome of any input as the user types.

## Morphing controls
- **Price button → stepper**: «12.20€ +» turns into dark «− 1 +», quantity overlay appears on the image (Chili: Ganbei). Principle: the control transforms in place instead of opening something.
- **Central «+» → «×»** opening two large action tiles over a dim overlay (Chili: Balcia). Principle: one entry point, two jobs, no menu.
- **Disabled CTA in a desaturated brand colour** instead of grey (Chili: Balcia, Rover, Twino) — keeps the brand feel while clearly inactive.

## Surfaces and depth
- **Photo + dark 54% overlay + noise texture (soft light 40%)** behind a step header, content on a rounded surface rising over it (Chili: Balcia). Principle: texture adds warmth to dark UI.
- **Glass on dark**: white 5% fills, blurred tab bar rgba(15,15,15,.7) (Chili: Rover).
- **Tinted shadows** that match the background hue (warm brown on beige — Chili: ToTheRescue) or a soft primary-colour glow under the CTA (Chili: Rover).
- **Gradient fade behind the sticky CTA** instead of a hard bar (Chili: Twino).
- **Sub-brand colour per product area** — FLEXI wallet violet vs main blue, the whole screen re-tints (Chili: Twino). Principle: colour as navigation.

## Typography as hero
- **Huge light number** (42 Medium, −1 tracking) as the main element of money screens (Chili: Twino).
- **Wide tracking** on body text for a soft, airy food brand (Chili: Ganbei) vs **negative tracking** on titles for a precise fintech feel (Chili: Twino) — tracking sets the character.

## Information and status
- **Counts inside segmented chips** «Missing 27 / Completed 5» (Chili: ToTheRescue).
- **Live result count** on the filter CTA «Show 8 results» (Chili: Rover).
- **Countdown status chip** over a map «Location will be sent in 30s» (Chili: ToTheRescue).
- **Locked-content teaser**: part of the checklist visible, the rest faded + what unlocks it (Chili: Rover).
- **Reassuring error**: «you haven't been charged yet» (Chili: Balcia).

## Input beyond typing
- **Hold-to-record voice** inside a form for field users (Chili: ToTheRescue).
- **Chips instead of dropdowns** for 2–4 options, one question per block (Chili: Balcia).
- **Presets + calculator** combo: quick picks first, manual entry second (Chili: Ganbei weight).

## Delight, in moderation
- **3D emoji-style badges** for offers, «new», spicy, vegan (Chili: Ganbei) — one illustration style per product.
- **Stats card** «152 km walked» as identity in the profile (Chili: Rover).
- **Avatar picker** from a playful set instead of photo upload (Chili: Rover).
